# make-motion-graphics — designed graphic systems in motion

The genre: what a motion-design studio ships — kinetic typography where the words
ARE the visual, geometric shape systems that transform, grids that cascade, counters
that land, a transition vocabulary that ties scenes into one designed system. The
design system (type, shapes, palette, grid) is built FIRST; motion is the message.

**The medium is CODE-DRAWN GRAPHICS ONLY: type, geometric shapes, colour, grids.**
No photo cutouts, no image generation, no AI imagery. That was the old track's
mistake — cutout puppetry is collage, not motion graphics. Assets generated by a
model are banned in this track. The only "assets" are fonts (bundled) and the
palette. If a subject genuinely needs photography, route it to /make-motion-graphics
is wrong — route to /make-vox or /make-short.

## The engine: `remotion/src/lib/mgkit.tsx` (read it before writing scenes)

One motion-design system, every rule encoded:

- **Timing tokens**: `MGE` (named beziers — `standard`, `emphasized`, `decel`
  entrances, `accel` exits, `outExpo` landings, `outBack` overshoot) + `MGD`
  (contextual duration table in frames: micro 3 / press 5 / modal 8 / scene 12 /
  transit 14 / counter 36 / cascade max 45) + `MGS` (stagger presets: char 2,
  word 6, cell 3) + `exitDur(d)` (exits ≈ 45% of enters).
- **Kinetic type**: `<KineticText>` — atoms split line/word/char, reveals
  (rise/mask/blur/scale/slide), compound property offsets, `emph` words that get
  the accent + landing pulse. `<MaskReveal>` wipe-on lines. `<WeightPush>`
  weight-step emphasis. Char split ONLY for short impact text (≤ 12 chars/line).
- **Transitions**: `<TransWindow enter={spec} exit={spec}>` — shared-axis X/Y/Z,
  fade-through, wipe, iris. **Pass ONE spec object to scene A's `exit` AND scene
  B's `enter`** — outgoing and incoming move IN UNISON across the cut (that's
  what makes it feel designed). Pick 1-2 transition kinds per film and reuse.
- **Grids**: `<GridCascade>` (index-stagger 2-4f), `<SplitScreen>` (reveal along
  the split line, contents stagger per pane).
- **Data motion**: `<Counter>` (1-2s ease-out, tabular numerals), `<DataBar>`,
  `<DataArc>`, `<DrawStroke>` (self-drawing outlines), `<BigStat>` (the full
  stat moment: count + unit + label + source).
- **Principles as functions**: `anticipate`, `overshoot` (5-8%), `followThrough`,
  `idleBob` (held frames stay alive), `parallax`.
- **Order→chaos→order**: `<ChaosField>` — grid holds ≥30f, burst 8-15f, staggered
  resolve. The single most reliable "expensive" moment.
- **Furniture**: `<PhoneSafe>`, `MG_SAFE` (x 60-940, y 250-1500), `<KickerChip>`.
- **Palettes**: `MGP.ink` / `MGP.paper` / `MGP.studio` — 2-3 colours + neutrals,
  60-30-10. One accent = one meaning per film.
- **SFX cues**: `cue(landF, durF)` + `typeCues(...)` — whoosh DURING the move,
  hit AT the landing frame.

## The voice is the clock

VO (Edge TTS, user names the voice or default en-US-ChristopherNeural, rate −4%)
locks FIRST. One VO line per scene. Scene timing is cut from the gaps between
lines. Kinetic type is cued to the REAL word times (`vo.gen.ts`) — the payoff
word of each line gets `emph` and lands exactly on the spoken word. Numbers in
the VO (`BigStat`, `Counter`) start on the word that says them. Never re-time
the VO to fit visuals; re-time the picture.

## Format

1080×1920 @30fps (reel) or 1920×1080 (YouTube), ~25-45s, 4-8 scenes. Type sizes
@1080: display 110-170, sub 44-52, body ≥ 42, captions ≥ 48, source ≥ 26 — and
body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against its ACTUAL background (squint-test every frame).

## Stage 1 — script + design system (before any pixels)

1. The story: 4-8 VO lines, one idea per line, each line names ONE visual beat
   (a claim → its number, a turn → its shape system, a payoff → the chaos/resolution).
   Facts verified by web search; sources listed in script.md.
2. **Pick the design system** (this is what makes it motion GRAPHICS):
   - palette: one of `MGP.*` (or a 2-colour+neutrals custom in that shape) —
     write down what the accent MEANS ("gold = the payoff, teal = the system")
   - type: one display face (`FONT_IMPACT` for punch / `FONT_HEAVY` for
     statements / `FONT_DISPLAY` for tech) + `FONT_BODY` + `FONT_MONO` for data
   - the transition set: 1-2 kinds, reused film-wide
   - the shape language: rounded vs sharp, circle vs rect motif
3. Pick the arc template:
   - **A. Kinetic-type story reel** (a claim argued in words): the words ARE
     the visual — char-split hook, word-split body, one `ChaosField` of the
     key words at the turn, `WeightPush` payoff, resolve to the title lockup.
   - **B. Stat/infographic reel** (numbers tell the story): hook claim →
     `BigStat` counters land on the VO numbers → `DataBar`/`DataArc` compare →
     `GridCascade` of facts → closing stat + source.
   - **C. Brand/feature reel** (a product/launch): kicker chip + logo lockup
     (type-drawn, never an image) → feature `GridCascade` → `SplitScreen`
     before/after → payoff moment → hold.
4. Storyboard table in script.md: scene → time window → system/animation → emph
   words → transition into next → SFX cue. ONE confirmation round if the user is
   interactive; then lock. No animating before the storyboard exists.

## Stage 2 — scenes (one file per scene, in order)

Each scene: `<PhoneSafe>` + content + `<TransWindow enter/exit>`. Every animation
derives from `useCurrentFrame()` (vidtsx rules: no state, interpolate ranges
strictly increasing, `<Sequence>` local frames = global_from subtraction).

1. **The hook (0-3s)**: char-split impact headline from line 1, `emph` on the
   payoff word, lands ON the spoken word. Kicker chip if the film needs one.
2. **The body scenes**: ONE system each (grid / data / split / type), alternating
   composition type (full-bleed type → data layout → centered shape moment).
   Something new every 2-4s; held frames stay alive with `idleBob`.
3. **The turn**: `ChaosField` (order→chaos→order) or a full-frame `DrawStroke`
   arrow path on the recontextualization line.
4. **The payoff**: `BigStat` or the `WeightPush` word, held ≥ 30f. Silence in
   the mix just before it (drop the music bed, land the hit) — loudest moment
   ON the payoff, then decay. NO CTA outro — end on the lockup/last stat.

**Banned (the amateur tells this track exists to kill):** linear keyframes,
one duration everywhere, whole-block text, center-anchored scaling on everything,
simultaneous mass movement without stagger, full-spectrum palettes, off-grid
elements, chaos without resolution, fast unblurred moves, unreadable type at
phone size, CTA endings, "Thanks for watching".

## Stage 3 — QA + render (measured, per scene while building)

After each scene: `node scripts/frames.mjs MgN<Scene> <cues> --scale=0.5`, READ
the PNGs with VLM: words readable over their actual background · atoms landed
(not mid-cascade at cue frames) · nothing colliding · contrast passes squint
test · on mute, the type + stats carry the story. Master: `npm run gen` +
`npx remotion render MgN<Name> out/MgN<Name>.mp4`. Measured: `tools/qa_frozen_time.sh`
(≤ ~1s per 30s), `tools/qa_determinism.sh MgN<Name>` (same frame twice = same
pixels — mgkit is seeded; a failure means YOU introduced state), `tools/qa_loudness.sh`.
Flagships: `/review-cut` fresh-critic round.

## Stage 4 — voice + sound

```
python tools/gen_voice_edge.py --beats mg-reels/mg-N-<slug>/beats.json \
    --voice en-US-AndrewNeural --emit-ts remotion/src/shots/mg-N/vo.gen.ts \
    --mux remotion/out/MgN<Name>.mp4
```
Re-time emph words + counter starts to the REAL word times (the words always
win). SFX per the cue map (whoosh during the move, hit AT the landing — `mgkit`
exports the landing frames): soft whooshes on transitions · ticks on counter
digits (one per ~10% not per digit) · ONE riser into the chaos burst · a
settle/sub hit on the resolve · low ambience bed that DROPS for the payoff
silence. Library-first (`suggest-sfx`), web-search (Mixkit/Pixabay) when the
library misses. Music: minimal pulse/bed, hard-ducked under VO. Voice ≥ music;
if a cue fights a word, the cue loses.

## Done =

Design system locked BEFORE pixels · VO locked before animation · kinetic type
word-exact on real word times · one constant visual identity film-wide (palette
+ type + transition vocabulary) · 1-2 transition kinds, used consistently ·
every move on named easing with contextual durations · order→chaos→order beat
present (if the arc calls for it) · stats land on their spoken numbers with
sources on screen · measured QA all green · no CTA outro — end on the payoff.
