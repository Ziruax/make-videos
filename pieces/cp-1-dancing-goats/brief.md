# cp-1-dancing-goats — "The Bean That Woke the World"

`/make-cut-paper` example piece. 9:16, 37.7s @ 24fps, 120 BPM, motion on 2s,
voice-driven (Edge TTS + faster-whisper word times).

## Spec

- **The story:** a history of coffee in six full-bleed paper worlds — the Kaldi
  legend (Ethiopia), the Yemeni monastery nights, London's penny universities,
  the 1727 seedling smuggled into Brazil, the bloom, and today's two billion
  cups — returning to the hillside it started on, changed.
- **Format:** F2 morph chain — 5 short shape-morph bridges (d: 0.4) + ONE hard
  cut (the slam on the payoff), the pattern `examples/history-of-ai` proved.
- **Style:** cut-paper. Torn edges, drop shadows, crayon, patterns, hand-lettered
  tags. One dominant wall colour per era so the chronology reads on a contact
  sheet: sky → ochre → tweed → navy → green → yellow → sky.
- **The device (sayable):** the spark — the coffee's buzz, the ONLY orange —
  gains a ray everywhere coffee lands (5 in Ethiopia → 10 by today), and the
  coffee thread carries object by object: **cherry → bean → pot → urn …
  cup → bouquet … cherry → cup → cherry** (the last bridge loops the piece).
- **The turn:** the heist era drops to a navy night; the seedling sprouts in
  **1.3s of scored silence**; the slam (the only hard cut, on the 8th grid) is
  the single loudest hit of the piece.

## Claims + sources (web-checked this run)

- Kaldi, the 9th-century Ethiopian goatherd whose goats danced — presented as
  **legend**; Wikipedia "Kaldi"; cogcoffee.com; dancinggoats.com.
- Coffee cultivation and the brew begin with 15th-century Sufi monks in Yemen
  (night prayers; the port of Mokha) — hamdancoffee.com; Britannica "Mokha";
  perfectdailygrind.com.
- England's first coffeehouse: Oxford, 1650/1652; London coffeehouses nicknamed
  "penny universities" (a penny's admission) — historic-uk.com; Wikipedia
  "English coffeehouses".
- 1727: Francisco de Melo Palheta obtains coffee seedlings in Cayenne (French
  Guiana) — the bouquet story is the standard account, phrased on screen as
  "charmed" — baristahustle.com (CBGB 0.04).
- ~2.25 billion cups a day; Brazil grows about a third of the world's coffee —
  verenastreet.com (consumption statistics, 2025); International Coffee
  Organization via usafacts.org.

## Sound plan

The arc: soft morning pad (legend) → warm drone + hand-drum heartbeat (monks) →
busy 8th-note groove + mug clinks (London) → tense drone that dries up (the
heist) → **true silence, then the slam: stab + sub + chime, the loudest** →
the groove swells back under "Today" (never louder than the slam) → the opening
pad, decaying, + the waking motif (the end is the start). SFX: paper swishes on
every morph, hoof ticks, fire crackle, chalk scratch, cup clinks, one goat-mug
clink, the title's pencil scratch. Master −14 LUFS, music −14 dB under the voice.
