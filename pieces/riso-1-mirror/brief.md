# riso-1-mirror — "The Mirror Wave"

`/make-riso` example piece. 9:16, 27.8s @ 24fps, 120 BPM, motion on 2s,
voice-driven (Edge TTS + faster-whisper word times, 57/57 heard).

## Spec

- **The story:** how noise cancelling actually works, printed in three inks.
  The engine drone (pink waves) washes the cabin; the earcup's microphone
  hears it; a chip flips the wave upside down (the teal mirror); the speaker
  plays the mirror at the noise; every peak meets a trough; they cancel —
  and the cancellation is drawn as the **knockout to paper**, the medium's
  own white. The piece performs its own subject: the synthesized engine
  drone hard-stops at the slam and never comes back.
- **Format:** 6 eras, 2 bridges (into the earcup close-up; home to the quiet
  cabin) + 3 cuts on the 8th grid. K3 is THE SLAM — 0.6s of scored silence
  before it, the single loudest hit.
- **Style:** riso — nothing painted, everything printed. Halftone screens at
  three angles, misregistration, ink dropouts. Colour code as plate tones:
  **the noise = pink, the mirror = teal, silence = paper** (knockouts); the
  hero = the ink-blue bean in headphones. The wave ribbons print at tone
  0.62 — the dots ARE the wave texture.
- **The device (sayable):** the headphone answers every wave with its mirror,
  and where they disagree the paper wins.

## Claims + sources

- ANC: a microphone measures incoming noise; the electronics generate a wave
  with the same amplitude and inverted phase (antiphase); the speaker emits
  it; superposition cancels the original — destructive interference
  (Wikipedia "Active noise control"; soundcore.com and srhythm.com
  explainers). Works best on continuous low-frequency noise like engine
  drone — hence "almost perfectly" (mids and highs leak; the piece shows the
  ideal case at the ear).
- No plane-specific figures are claimed.

## Sound plan

The engine drone (A1 + E2 + a lowpassed noise bed) runs from frame one and
**hard-stops at 16.85** — the pre-slam silence (0.6s) is the piece's own
noise cancelling. The cabin theme tries to exist over it; the mirror's motif
(a clean falling third D5→B4) enters at the flip; both-play adds a second
drone a whisker off frequency (the beat) + accelerating 8ths + a riser; then
silence; THE SLAM (stab + sub + chime, loudest); the relief pad swells in
behind it; the quiet cabin theme decays; the motif twice more. −14.0 LUFS.
